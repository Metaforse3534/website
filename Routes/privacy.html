This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / Controller

Orbit Systems B.V. is the controller for account, website, billing, support, and product usage data unless a signed DPA states that Orbit acts as processor.

002 / Data We Process

We may process account identifiers, contact details, authentication records, device and log data, billing metadata, support messages, prompts, files, outputs, workflow metadata, and cookie preferences.

003 / Purposes

We process data to provide Orbit, secure accounts, prevent abuse, process payments, respond to support requests, comply with law, and improve reliability and product quality.

004 / Legal Bases

Depending on context, processing relies on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, legal obligations, or another lawful basis available under GDPR.

005 / AI Content

Prompts, uploaded files, code, and outputs are processed to deliver requested AI features, troubleshoot errors, enforce abuse safeguards, and maintain service reliability.

006 / Processors

We may use service providers for hosting, authentication, payments, analytics, email, support, security monitoring, and AI infrastructure. Providers are bound by contractual safeguards.

007 / International Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, Orbit uses appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent legal mechanisms.

008 / Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for service delivery, account management, security, legal compliance, billing, dispute resolution, and backup cycles.

009 / Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent where applicable.

010 / Requests

Privacy requests can be sent to support@orbitdev.org. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

011 / Children

Orbit is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by anyone below the legal minimum age in their jurisdiction.

012 / Changes

We may update this policy as Orbit changes. Material updates will be reflected by the effective date on this page.
