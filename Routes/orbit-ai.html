What Orbit AI is

Orbit AI is the intelligent workspace from Orbit Systems B.V. It brings together AI chat, browser automation, coding assistance, research, file analysis, voice workflows, and collaboration in one environment.

Current public version: 0.0.20. Orbit is in active development. Features are documented as they ship; limitations are acknowledged.

Capabilities available today

  • AI conversations for analysis, writing, reasoning, and research
  • Coding assistance for writing, reviewing, and debugging
  • File understanding and document synthesis
  • Browser automation for repetitive web tasks
  • AI agent workflows with human oversight
  • Workflow automation across knowledge work
  • Developer-oriented tooling and documentation

Pro Pulsar

Pro Pulsar is Orbit’s premium workspace plan for priority model access and advanced agent workflows. It is distinct from Pulsar LLM research and Pulsar V1 robotics.

Privacy and trust

Orbit does not collect user data for advertising. The company operates under European privacy law, including GDPR, and publishes privacy, security, and usage policies.
