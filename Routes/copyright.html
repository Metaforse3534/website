Copyright Policy

How Orbit handles copyright concerns, takedown requests, repeat infringement, and user content disputes.

Effective: July 6, 2026Orbit Systems B.V.Contact: support@orbitdev.org
This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / Respect for Rights

Users must only upload, generate, share, or automate content they have the right to use.

002 / Infringement Notices

Copyright owners can send notices to support@orbitdev.org identifying the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing material, contact information, and a good-faith statement.

003 / Removal

Orbit may remove or restrict content, disable links, suspend accounts, or preserve records where appropriate.

004 / Counter Notices

Users who believe material was removed by mistake may send a counter notice with sufficient information for review.

005 / Repeat Infringers

Orbit may terminate or restrict accounts that repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights.

006 / AI Outputs

AI-generated outputs can raise copyright questions. Users are responsible for reviewing outputs and ensuring they have rights needed for their use case.

007 / No Legal Advice

Orbit does not provide legal advice about copyright ownership, fair use, licensing, or clearance.
