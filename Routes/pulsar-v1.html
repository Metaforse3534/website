An agile reconnaissance and support unit concept built for dynamic environments — designed for speed, precision, and integration with human teams.

Concept overview

Pulsar V1 is Orbit AI’s vision for a mobile robotic platform: matte structural bodywork, bronze interaction surfaces, and a Pulsar Core for onboard computation and power distribution — designed to work alongside people, not replace them.

Pulsar V1 is not available for purchase. It is an in-development research and engineering concept. Do not treat renders or target specs as verified performance of a built unit.
