Refund Policy

The refund and cancellation rules for Orbit subscriptions, credits, desktop installers, and paid add-ons.

Effective: July 6, 2026Orbit Systems B.V.Contact: support@orbitdev.org
This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / Subscriptions

You can cancel future renewals from account settings or support. Cancellation does not automatically refund the current billing period.

002 / Refund Window

Unless checkout terms say otherwise, refund requests for a first subscription payment should be made within 14 days of purchase.

003 / Usage-Based Charges

Consumed credits, metered usage, paid API calls, or third-party infrastructure costs may be non-refundable.

004 / Renewals

Renewal refunds are reviewed case by case and may be declined when the service was used after renewal.

005 / Add-Ons

One-time add-ons, implementation services, priority support, and enterprise onboarding may have separate refund terms in the applicable order form.

006 / Abuse

Orbit may deny refunds for accounts suspended for fraud, chargeback abuse, policy violations, or unlawful activity.

007 / How To Request

Send refund requests to support@orbitdev.org with the account email, payment date, invoice reference if available, and reason for the request.

008 / Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method when possible. Payment processors and banks control final posting times.
