About Orbit AI

Orbit AI is building intelligent systems, AI assistants, developer tools, and language model technology — with engineering depth and a clear roadmap.

Who we are

Orbit AI is developed by Orbit Systems B.V., a technology company registered in the Netherlands. We publish public documentation, policies, and a public code repository so people can verify how we operate.

What we build

We build the Orbit AI workspace — chat, automation, coding assistance, research, files, agents, and collaboration. We pursue Pro Pulsar language model and infrastructure research, and Pulsar V1 as a longer-term robotics vision — labeled honestly by status.

Why we exist

Most AI tools are built to be impressive. Orbit is built to be dependable. Capability without accountability creates software that is hard to trust in real work. We exist to close that gap.

Our vision

A future where AI serves as a reliable extension of human intelligence: present when needed, honest about what it knows, respectful of data, and useful across software and — over a long horizon — physical environments.

Our principles

  • Honesty about capabilities and limits
  • Privacy by design and GDPR-aligned practices
  • Security as an engineering standard
  • Human oversight of consequential actions
  • Transparency through published policies and documentation

Our research

Research directions include workspace intelligence, Pro Pulsar model technology, and long-term robotics (Pulsar V1). See the research page for status-labeled initiatives.
