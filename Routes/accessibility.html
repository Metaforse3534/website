Accessibility Statement

Orbit’s accessibility commitments for websites, desktop experiences, documentation, and support.

Effective: July 6, 2026Orbit Systems B.V.Contact: support@orbitdev.org
This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / Commitment

Orbit aims to make its websites and product surfaces usable by people with diverse abilities, devices, and assistive technologies.

002 / Standards

We use WCAG principles as a practical guide for perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust interfaces.

003 / Current Work

Orbit reviews contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states, semantic markup, responsive layouts, readable text, and reduced-motion behavior as the product evolves.

004 / Known Limits

Some AI-generated previews, third-party embeds, experimental interfaces, and desktop automation surfaces may not yet meet every accessibility target.

005 / Feedback

Send accessibility feedback to support@orbitdev.org with the page or feature, assistive technology used, browser or device, and the issue encountered.

006 / Response

Orbit will review accessibility reports and prioritize fixes based on severity, reach, feasibility, and product roadmap.

007 / Alternate Access

If a feature is blocking your access, contact support and we will try to provide the information or service through another reasonable channel.
