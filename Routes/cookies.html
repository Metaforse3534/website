Cookie Policy

How Orbit uses cookies and similar technologies on its websites and connected services.

Effective: July 6, 2026Orbit Systems B.V.Contact: support@orbitdev.org
This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / What Cookies Are

Cookies are small browser storage files. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, device identifiers, and session tokens.

002 / Essential Cookies

Essential cookies support login, security, load balancing, fraud prevention, cookie preferences, and basic site functionality.

003 / Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand site performance, navigation patterns, errors, and product interest. Where required, we ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

004 / Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices such as theme, cookie banner state, language, and product settings.

005 / Marketing Cookies

Orbit may use marketing cookies only where legally permitted and subject to consent where required.

006 / Third Parties

Service providers may set cookies for hosting, payments, analytics, security, support, or embedded content.

007 / Managing Cookies

You can use the cookie banner, browser settings, or device settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may break parts of Orbit.

008 / Retention

Cookie lifetimes vary from session-only storage to longer-lived preferences. We limit retention to what is appropriate for the cookie purpose.

009 / Updates

We may update this policy when our cookie use changes.
