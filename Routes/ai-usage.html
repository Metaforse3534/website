AI Usage Policy

Rules for responsible use of Orbit AI features, model connections, generated content, and automated workflows.

Effective: July 6, 2026Orbit Systems B.V.Contact: support@orbitdev.org
This page is provided for product transparency and general compliance information. Enterprise customers may have additional or superseding terms in a signed order form, DPA, or service agreement.

001 / Human Responsibility

You are responsible for prompts, instructions, files, tool calls, automated actions, and decisions made from AI outputs.

002 / No High-Risk Decisions

Do not use Orbit as the sole basis for decisions involving health, legal rights, employment, credit, housing, insurance, public benefits, education access, or safety-critical systems.

003 / Verification

Review and test outputs before shipping code, sending messages, publishing content, executing workflows, or relying on analysis.

004 / Prohibited AI Use

Do not use Orbit to create malware, evade security controls, exploit systems, impersonate people, generate deceptive content, facilitate violence, or violate privacy rights.

005 / Data Rights

Only upload data, code, files, images, or credentials that you have the right to process with Orbit and connected AI providers.

006 / Automation

Automations must respect rate limits, robots rules, platform terms, consent requirements, and applicable laws.

007 / Model Providers

Some AI features depend on third-party model providers. Their availability, safety systems, and terms may affect outputs and feature behavior.

008 / Safety Enforcement

Orbit may limit, block, or review activity to prevent abuse, protect users, and comply with law.
