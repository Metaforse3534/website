Why We Are Building Pulsar

By Orbit AI 2026-07-20 6 min read

Editorial / placeholder essay — not a fabricated news event or research result announcement.

Software products that depend on language models inherit both their strengths and their failure modes. Orbit AI is building Pulsar as a research initiative so we can understand those trade-offs from the inside — not so we can announce a finished model before it exists.

Ambition with labels

Pulsar covers foundation models, training infrastructure, inference, and planned developer APIs. Each area is labeled Research, Prototype, or Planned. We do not publish invented benchmarks.

Connection to the workspace

The Orbit workspace already routes work across models. Pulsar research is a longer horizon: deeper ownership of the intelligence layer that powers software, agents, and eventually physical systems.

What this is not

This essay is not a launch announcement. It is a statement of direction. When Pulsar reaches a verifiable milestone, we will say so clearly — and not before.
