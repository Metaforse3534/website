Inside Orbit AI's AI Architecture

By Orbit AI 2026-07-12 7 min read

Editorial / placeholder essay — not a fabricated news event or research result announcement.

Orbit AI is not built around a single model. The workspace is designed to connect capable models to tools people already need: files, browsers, coding contexts, and multi-step agents.

Composable tools

Architecture favors composability over lock-in. Features ship when they are ready enough for real use, with honest versioning — currently public version 0.0.20.

Reliability as a goal

Uptime, consistent behavior, and graceful errors matter more than flashy demos. Engineering quality is part of the product promise.

Research boundary

Workspace shipping surfaces are separate from Pulsar research. Confusing the two would overclaim. We keep them distinct on purpose.
