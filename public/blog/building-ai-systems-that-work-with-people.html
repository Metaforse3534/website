Building AI Systems That Work With People

By Orbit AI 2026-07-15 4 min read

Editorial / placeholder essay — not a fabricated news event or research result announcement.

AI systems are most useful when they amplify human judgment rather than obscure it. Orbit’s product design keeps people in control of consequential actions.

Oversight by default

Automations and agents should be understandable. Users should know what a workflow will do before it does something that cannot be undone.

Honest limits

Models are uncertain. Interfaces should communicate uncertainty instead of performing confidence. That is how people learn when to rely on the system — and when not to.
